# PhotoPro Feature Guide

## 1. Professional Photo Editing Studio
- **Multi-Layer Engine:** Add, duplicate, delete, reorder, lock, and hide layers. Blend modes include Normal, Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Darken, Lighten, Color Dodge, Color Burn, Hard Light, Soft Light, Difference, and Exclusion.
- **Lightroom Color Suite:**
  - Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks.
  - Temperature, Tint, Vibrance, Saturation, Clarity, Dehaze.
  - 8-Channel HSL Mixer for Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Cyan, Blue, Purple, Magenta.
- **3D LUT Support:** Real-time `.cube` 3D LUT file parser and 3D trilinear interpolation color grading.
- **Retouch Suite:** AI Skin Smoothing with bilateral edge preservation, Yellow-suppression teeth whitening, and localized blemish removal.
- **Creative Effects:** Pencil sketch, oil painting, comic cell-shading, chromatic aberration glitch, and duotone gradients.
- **Interactive Tools:** Freehand brush, eraser, clone stamp with sampling crosshair, spot healing, shape generator, text overlay, sticker library.
- **History & Recovery:** Undo/redo stack and auto-save recovery.

## 2. Non-Linear Video Studio
- **Multi-Track Timeline:** Video lane, Audio lane, Subtitle & text overlay lane.
- **Editing Primitives:** Split at playhead, trim in/out points, speed control (0.25x to 4x).
- **Audio Studio:** Real-time Web Audio API frequency synthesis (Whoosh, Click, Pop, Beep) and live microphone voiceover recording.
- **Chroma Key:** Real-time green screen removal with threshold, tolerance, and custom replacement background.
- **Transitions & Aspect Ratios:** Crossfade, Slide, Wipe, Zoom transitions; 16:9, 9:16 (Shorts/Reels), 1:1, 4:5, 21:9 aspect ratios.
- **Export:** Browser canvas recorder exporting up to 4K resolution WebM/MP4.

## 3. PDF Studio
- **Images to PDF:** Batch photo conversion to multi-page PDF documents.
- **Merge & Split:** Combine documents or extract custom page intervals.
- **Page Manager:** Reorder pages with visual drag & drop and delete unwanted pages.
- **Compressor:** Re-sample images within PDFs to reduce document file size.
- **Stamper & Editor:** Add dynamic watermarks, page numbers, and custom header text.
- **Conversion Suite:** High-resolution PDF to PNG/JPEG rasterization, text/word document extraction.
- **Security & E-Sign:** Draw digital signatures and encrypt documents with AES passwords.
- **Templates:** Instant generator for professional invoices, resumes, and certificates.

## 4. AI & Utility Tools
- **Background Remover:** Client-side neural segmentation with manual refine brush.
- **Image Converter & Compressor:** Instant format conversion and quality optimization.
- **Palette Extractor:** Dominant hex palette generator with one-click clipboard copy.
